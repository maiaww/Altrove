About ALTROVE

Every place tells a story — this is where I keep mine.

I'm Maia, a young traveller from Perth, Western Australia. ALTROVE is where I keep my stories from the road — the lessons hidden inside them, and the photographs that bring them back to life. It started with a simple belief: that travel isn't just about seeing beautiful places, but about understanding different ways of life.

These are honest accounts from the road — the good days and the hard ones, the bruised knees and the lifelong friends. If they inspire you to take your own leap, then they've done their job. For collaborations, photography or a chat, you'll always find me a message away.
